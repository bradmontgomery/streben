Why Streben?

Fitness platforms are built to keep you scrolling. Streben is built to help you understand your riding.

🔒

Own your data

Years of riding are worth more than a feed. Streben keeps your whole history, every ride and every second of power and heart rate, on your computer. No subscription, no account, and nothing lost if a service changes its rules.

🤫

Quiet by design

No followers, no kudos, no leaderboards, no ads. Just you and your training, without the pressure of performing it for anyone else.

🔗

One history, every source

Ride with a Wahoo, share to Strava, keep the odd Garmin file? Streben brings them together and recognizes when two services recorded the same ride, so nothing is counted twice.

The bigger picture

Effort out. Fuel in.

Most training apps stop when the ride does. Streben also reads your food log and lines it up, day by day, with the energy you spent on the bike.

See whether you are under-fuelling your biggest weeks, how your weight moves with your training, and what a season really asks of you. Anything Streben has to estimate, like the snacks you ate mid-ride, is clearly marked as an estimate.

Who it's for

  • 🚴 Cyclists who love their numbers and want to dig into them on their own terms.
  • 🌿 Riders stepping back from social fitness apps without giving up their history.
  • 📓 Anyone who wants a private, permanent training log that no one can switch off.

Get started

Streben runs on your own computer and you use it in your web browser. Setup takes a few minutes: download it, connect Strava or Wahoo (or drop in your .fit files), and watch your history fill in.
